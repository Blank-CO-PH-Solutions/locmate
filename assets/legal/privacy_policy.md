# Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Blank CO PH Solutions ("we", "us") operates the Locmate app (the "Service"). This Privacy Policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and the rights you have. It follows the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), its Implementing Rules and Regulations, and the issuances of the National Privacy Commission (NPC).

By creating an account or using Locmate, you acknowledge this Policy. Where the law requires consent, we ask for it in the app, and you may withdraw it at any time.

## 1. Information we collect

**Account information.** Your name, email address and password (stored only as a secure hash). If you use Google sign-in, we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier from Google.

**Location information.** Your device's precise location (latitude, longitude, accuracy, heading and speed) while location sharing is turned on, including when the app is in the background. You can pause sharing at any time in Settings.

**Device information.** Your battery level and charging status, used to show your circle when your phone is low on battery, plus basic technical data needed to run the Service (app version, operating system, IP address).

**Content you create.** Circles you create or join, places (saved locations such as "Home" or "School"), chat messages, and alerts such as arrivals, departures, low battery and SOS.

**Purchase information.** If you subscribe to Premium, the App Store or Google Play processes your payment. We never receive your card details. We receive a purchase identifier, product, store and expiry date through our subscription provider, RevenueCat.

## 2. How we use your information

- To provide the core features: showing your location to members of your circle, place arrival and departure alerts, battery alerts, SOS alerts and chat.
- To create and secure your account and authenticate you.
- To manage subscriptions and enforce plan features.
- To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse, and fix problems.
- To comply with legal obligations.

We do **not** sell your personal information, and we do not use your location for advertising.

## 3. Who can see your information

- **Your circle.** Members of your circle can see your name, live location (while sharing is on), battery level, places, chat messages and alerts. You choose who joins your circle by sharing its invite code.
- **Service providers** who process data for us under contract:
  - Supabase (database, authentication and realtime hosting)
  - RevenueCat (subscription management)
  - Apple and Google (app distribution, payments and Google sign-in)
  - OpenStreetMap tile servers (map images; these receive your IP address and the map area you are viewing)
- **Authorities**, when required by law, a court order, or to protect someone's life or safety.

## 4. International transfers

Our service providers may store or process data outside the Philippines. When they do, we require safeguards that give your data protection comparable to the Data Privacy Act.

## 5. How long we keep information

- Your live location is overwritten with each update. We keep only your most recent position.
- Chat messages, places and alerts are kept while your circle exists.
- When you delete your account, we delete your profile, location and membership data right away. Records we must keep by law (for example purchase records) are kept only as long as required.

## 6. Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to:

- be informed about how your data is processed;
- access your personal data;
- object to processing, or withdraw consent;
- correct inaccurate data;
- erase or block your data;
- data portability;
- claim damages; and
- file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph).

You can edit your name, pause location sharing, leave your circle, or **delete your account** at any time in **Settings**. For any other request, contact us using the details below.

## 7. Security

We protect your data with encryption in transit (TLS), row-level access rules that limit data to members of your circle, and secure password hashing. No system is perfectly secure, so please also keep your device and password safe.

## 8. Children

Locmate is not intended for children under 13 without the consent and supervision of a parent or guardian. A parent or guardian who sets up Locmate for a minor is responsible for that use and consents on the minor's behalf.

## 9. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Policy from time to time. If the changes are significant, we will tell you in the app before they take effect. The effective date at the top always shows the latest version.
